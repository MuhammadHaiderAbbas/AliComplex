Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about booking, fees and your visit.

Appointments

How do I book an appointment?

Click “Book Appointment”, choose a doctor, pick a date and time, and enter the patient’s details. You get a booking reference straight away. You can also call us or send a WhatsApp message.

Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

Your time slot is reserved as soon as you book. Our staff may call you on the number you gave to confirm. You can check the status any time on the “My Booking” page.

How far ahead can I book?

You can book up to 60 days ahead, on the days and times each doctor is available.

Can I cancel or change my appointment?

Yes. Go to “My Booking”, enter your reference and phone number, and cancel. Then book a new time, or call us for help.

I lost my booking reference. What should I do?

Call or WhatsApp us with the patient’s name and phone number and our staff will find your booking.

Can I book for my child or a family member?

Yes. Enter the patient’s name and age, and your own phone number so we can contact you.

Fees & payment

How much is the consultation fee?

Each doctor’s fee is shown on their profile and when you book. You can also call us to ask.

How do I pay?

The fee is paid at the hospital reception at the time of your visit.

Are there extra charges for booking online?

No. Booking online is free.

Your visit

What should I bring?

Please bring your CNIC (or the child’s B-form), previous prescriptions and test reports, and a list of medicines you take.

When should I arrive?

Please arrive about 10 minutes before your appointment time.

Where is the hospital?

Chungi No. 1, Suraj Miani Road, Multan. Use the “Get Directions” link on the Contact page to open Google Maps.

Reviews & feedback

How can I leave a review?

After your visit is marked complete, open “My Booking”, enter your reference and phone number, and rate your doctor. Reviews are checked by our staff before they appear on the website.

How do I make a complaint or suggestion?

Please use the form on the Contact page. Our staff read every message.

How can I check that a salary slip or letter from the hospital is genuine?

Every salary slip and letter we issue has a verification code at the bottom. Enter it on the “Verify a Document” page of this website.
