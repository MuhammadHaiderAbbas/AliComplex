Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Click “Book Appointment”, choose a doctor, pick a date and time, and enter the patient’s details. You will get a booking reference straight away. You can also call us on 061-2039965.

Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

Your slot is reserved as soon as you book. Our staff may call you on the number you gave to confirm. You can check the status anytime on the “My Booking” page.

How do I pay the consultation fee?

The fee is paid at the hospital reception at the time of your visit. Fees are shown on each doctor’s profile.

Can I cancel or change my appointment?

Yes. Go to “My Booking”, enter your reference number and phone number, and cancel. Then book a new slot, or call us for help.

What should I bring?

Please bring your CNIC (or the child’s B-form), any previous prescriptions and test reports, and a list of medicines you take.
