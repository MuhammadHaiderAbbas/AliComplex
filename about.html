About Ali Medical Complex

Specialist care close to home, on Suraj Miani Road, Multan.

Who we are

Ali Medical Complex is a healthcare facility at Chungi No. 1, Suraj Miani Road, Multan. We bring together specialists in eye care, ENT, cardiology, gynecology, dermatology, pediatrics and dentistry so that families can get expert care in one place.

Our doctors are supported by an allied health team (physiotherapy, nutrition, occupational therapy and psychology), a nursing team, an in-house laboratory under a pathologist, and a pharmacy run by a Pharm-D pharmacist. The hospital is led by our Director, with a Primary Health Organisation Manager overseeing day-to-day services.

We focus on careful diagnosis, clear explanations and respectful treatment, and we use online booking so patients spend less time waiting.
