About Ali Medical Complex

Specialist care close to home, on Suraj Miani Road, Multan.

Who we are

Ali Medical Complex is a healthcare facility at Chungi No. 1, Suraj Miani Road, Multan. We bring together qualified specialists in endocrinology, child health, internal medicine and psychology so that families can get expert outpatient care in one place.

Our doctors trained at leading institutions, including Nishtar Hospital Multan, Quaid-e-Azam Medical College and the College of Physicians & Surgeons Pakistan. Several hold postgraduate qualifications such as FCPS, MD and MS.

We focus on careful diagnosis, clear explanations and respectful treatment, and we use online booking so patients spend less time waiting.
