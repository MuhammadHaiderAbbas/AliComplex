Set up Telegram alerts
  1. Install Telegram on the phone that should get alerts.
  2. In Telegram, search @BotFather, tap Start, send /newbot, and choose a name (e.g. Ali Medical Alerts) and a username ending in bot.
  3. BotFather replies with a token like 123456789:AA…. Paste it below.
  4. Open your new bot in Telegram and press Start (or add the bot to your staff group and send any message there).
  5. Click Find my chat, tick the chats to alert, then Save and Send test.
